# Reading

1. Choosing papers to read:
   1. Choose a bunch of papers.
   2. Skim through them.
   3. Filter out bad papers.
2. Reading papers:
   1. Title/abstract/figures.
   2. Intro + concluson + figures + skim rest (skip related work).
   3. Read but skim/skip math.
   4. Whole thing but skip parts that don't make sense.

# Questions after

1. What did authors try to accomplish?
2. What were the key elements of the approach?
3. What can you use yourself?
4. What other references do you want to follow?
